Shadow Of Doubt

The book is just part five of a nine part series, the Robyn Hunter Mysteries, in which the protagonist is trying to solve and juggle two parrallel mysteries at the same time. One of the first mysteries is of a threatening, blood-stained and splattered package that was sent to her new substitute English teacher, Ms. Denholm. The other mystery is on the disappearance of her missing ex-boyfriend, Nick, and a cryptic, muddled phone call that she had supposedly recieved from his number. This book was written by Canadian author, Norah McClintock, and published by Scholastic Canada on June 1st, 2008, with a total of 224 pages. I liked this book a lot, and found it very interesting, because I am very into mystery fiction and non-fiction genres of books, especially since I was a little kid. I have been reading these kinds of books for a long time, and I am very into books with these kinds of genres because it gives you a sense of suspense, wondering what could happen next.

When it is very suspenseful like this book is, it makes you want to read the book further, to possibly see what the ending could be about, what clues they will solve, and what might come next. I believe that this book also helps the reader gain an interest in finishing the other nine books in the series as well, especially those after this one, to see what the resolution could be. So therefore, in conclusion, this is why the book "Shadow Of Doubt" is my best and favorite thing that I had read recently, and I plan on continuing the series as well so that I can get the proper resolution that I am looking for.
