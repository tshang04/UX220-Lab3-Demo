MICHAEL

This movie is basically about the life and career of a young and famous Michael Jackson. It is a biographical musical drama that explores his rise to fame and the challenges he faced throughout his career. This film chronicles almost the entire aspect of his entire life and career journey starting from his childhood in Gary, Indiana, to his rise as a solo superstar up to the late 1980s and 90s. This film was released a few months ago, in April of this year, on the 24th for the United States and Canada, and holding a 97% audience approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes.

I liked this movie a lot, because it went into a sort of deep dive into Michael Jackson's early childhood days, and how his superstardom began, as well as some other aspects of his career that might not have been as well known or documented. It also included some behind-the-scenes footage of his shows and performances, and created re-enacted versions of his dances and concerts too, which I really enjoyed along with the music that he had released. This film gave me a new view and perspective on his life and early career as a young child star, and what it was really like to be as famous and talented as he was in his lifetime.
