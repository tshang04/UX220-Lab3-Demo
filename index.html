The MICHAEL Movie

This movie is basically about the entire life and career of a young and famous Michael Jackson for most of the 20th century. It is a biographical musical drama that explores his rise to fame and the challenges he faced throughout his massive and very successful career. This film chronicles the entire aspect of his entire life and career journey, starting from his childhood in Gary, Indiana, to his rise as a solo superstar in the late 1980s and 90s as well. This film was released a few months ago, in the month of April of this year, on the 24th for the United States and Canada, and holding a 97% audience approval rating on Rotten Tomatoes too.

I liked this movie a lot, because it went into a sort of deep dive into Michael Jackson's early childhood days, and how his superstardom began, as well as some other aspects of his career that might not have been as well known or documented. It also included some behind-the-scenes footage of his shows and performances, and created re-enacted versions of his dances and concerts too, which I really enjoyed along with the music that he had released. This film gave me a new view and perspective on his life and early career as a young child star, and what it was really like to be as famous and talented as he was in his lifetime.
